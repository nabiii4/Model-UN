The Ritual

{{ copy('ritual.p1') }}

Every new member gets offered three things: paper, a laptop, or a chair to just sit. Then a blank sheet, and one question: who is the debate opponent that scares you most?

People drew huge mouths that never stopped talking, eyes that never blinked, a suit so perfect the shoes reflected light, and even Einstein's twin brother. Each drawing had a story behind it — a real person's fear.

We turned each drawing into a practice opponent. You practice one round. Then the screen tells you: {{ copy('handoff.text') }} A club member wrote that rule, because the website was keeping people on their screens. Now it's a warm-up, never a replacement.

Soon people got bored of their own nightmares and started practicing against each other's. That's how we learned what everyone found hard — and how we started helping each other.
