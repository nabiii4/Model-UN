Privacy, in plain words

  • What we keep: your first name (or a nickname), a scrambled version of your PIN, and your practice: nightmares, rounds, feedback, and fear meters.
  • What we never collect: email, last name, birthday, school ID, or location.
  • Photos: your drawing is cleaned on your device before it's sent (location data is removed), cleaned again on the server, and only kept if you save it.
  • Camera and microphone: always optional. Camera video for face features stays on your laptop. Audio is never stored.
  • AI: the club's AI runs through our server. Your drawing and what you type are sent to the AI to make your practice opponent and its replies.
  • No ads, no trackers. Club Pulse counts are totals only — never who did what.
  • Shared Chromebooks: with "This is a shared Chromebook" checked, you're logged out when the browser closes or after 30 quiet minutes.
  • Your choice: on My Journey you can download all your data or delete it.

Questions? Ask a club officer, or use “Suggest a change.”
